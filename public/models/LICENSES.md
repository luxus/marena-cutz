# Model licenses

## head.glb

Infinite-Realities 3D head scan by Lee Perry-Smith.

Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0). Commercial use is allowed with credit.

- Scan: Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities, based on a work at triplegangers.com
- Convenient formats: Morgan McGuire and Guedis Cardenas, Williams College
- Source mirror used for the mesh and skin maps: [Vinc3r/graphic-assets](https://github.com/Vinc3r/graphic-assets/tree/master/3D-models/Lee-Perry-Smith-Head) (`head.glb`, lambertian colour, low-res normal)
- This repo: one subdivision level, smooth shading, normal strength restored, JPEG textures for the web. The skin fade and peach lineup are a runtime shader, not part of the scan.

## scissors.glb, clipper.glb, chair.glb

Original product meshes modeled in Blender for this site (shears with blades, pivot, and finger rings; clipper with body, toothed blade, taper lever, switch, and cord; hydraulic shop chair with chrome base, foot ring, padded seat, back, and headrest).

License: CC0 1.0. No third-party mesh is included in these three files.
